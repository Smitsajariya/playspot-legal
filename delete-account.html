Account Deletion

Delete your PlaySport account

You can request deletion of your PlaySport: Sports & Social account and its associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to playspotservices@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (the one you used with Google Sign-In).
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my PlaySport account".
  3. We will confirm and process your request, typically within 7 days.

What gets deleted

DeletedProfile info (name, photo, bio), posts, photos, hosted games/events, chat messages, location history
Retained (if legally required)Minimal records we are required to keep for fraud prevention or legal compliance, kept only as long as the law requires
TimeframeData is deleted within 30 days of a confirmed request

Questions about this process can also be sent to playspotservices@gmail.com.
