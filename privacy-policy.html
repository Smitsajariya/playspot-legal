Privacy Policy

PlaySport: Sports & Social

Effective date: September 29, 2026  ·  Package: com.smit.playsport

PlaySport ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile app that helps people find and join local sports games and events, and connect with other players. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. PlaySport is intended for users aged 18 and older.

1Information we collect

  • Account & profile information — your name, email address, and profile photo, whether entered directly or provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Content you create — posts, comments, photos, and games or events you host, shared to your profile or the social feed.
  • Location — with your permission, your approximate or precise location, used to show nearby games, venues and events on the map. You can deny or revoke this at any time in your device settings.
  • Messages — chat messages you send to other users through the app, delivered via our real-time messaging service.
  • Usage & device data — basic technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and crash or diagnostic logs, used to keep the app working reliably.

2How we use this information

  • To operate core features: matching you with nearby games, displaying the social feed, and enabling chat between users.
  • To maintain your profile and let other users discover and connect with you.
  • To keep the app secure, diagnose problems, and improve performance.
  • To communicate with you about your account when necessary.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3How information is stored & shared

Your data is stored on our backend servers and with the following service providers, solely to provide the app's functionality:

  • Google Sign-In — for secure account authentication.
  • Cloudinary — for hosting profile and post photos.
  • Our application backend (hosted on Render) — for storing profile, game, and chat data.

These providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

4Your choices

  • Edit or update your profile information at any time from within the app.
  • Turn off location access from your device's app permissions; some features that depend on location may no longer work.
  • Delete your account and data by emailing us at the address below with your account's email address; we will delete your data within a reasonable time, except where we are required to retain it by law.

5Children's privacy

PlaySport is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
